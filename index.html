    Our Story

    We were two college students in an economics class at Bonoan Building, 4th floor. (The same building behind our photo!)

    Raymund was fascinated by Yhumi’s eagerness during recitation. The very reason why he wanted to befriend her. Fortunately, Raymund was a Computer Science student, whose expertise happened to complement Yhumi’s weaknesses perfectly.

    Unbeknownst to them, their college friendship is the start of their stories as they navigate life - each pursuing individual careers and growing as a person separately. In years living with separate directions, their paths would, in time, cross once more, knowing each other more deeply and continuing friendship to a lifetime journey as a couple.
